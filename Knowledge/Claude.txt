Claude Opus 5.5 — The most capable model, designed for the hardest, most complex tasks: deep reasoning, 
nuanced writing, difficult coding problems, multi-step analysis. 
It's slower and more expensive to run, so it makes sense when quality matters more than speed.


Claude Sonnet 5 — The balanced option (what's answering you right now). 
Strong performance across most tasks — coding, writing, 
analysis — at a fraction of Opus's cost and with faster responses. 
This is usually the default choice for everyday use.


Claude Haiku 4.5 — The fastest and lightest model. 
Built for tasks that need quick responses at scale — simple queries, classification, 
high-volume applications where latency and cost matter more than squeezing out the absolute best reasoning.

Claude Fable 5.1 — Part of a newer tier above Opus called Mythos. 
Fable is meant for broader use with extra safety guardrails around sensitive 
domains like biology, cybersecurity, and AI research itself.
